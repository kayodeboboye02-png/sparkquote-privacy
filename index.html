SparkQuote Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

1. Overview

SparkQuote is a daily motivation and reflection app. We respect your privacy and this policy explains what we collect.

2. Data We Collect and Store Locally

SparkQuote stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • First name you enter during onboarding (to personalize greetings)
  • Reflection entries, favorite quotes, mood selections, streaks, and progress

This data is saved using shared_preferences on your device. It never leaves your device and is not uploaded to our servers. We have no access to it.

3. Advertising - Google AdMob

SparkQuote uses Google AdMob to display ads. To provide and improve its advertising services, AdMob may automatically collect and use certain information, including:

  • Your device's Google Advertising ID
  • IP address
  • Device information (device model, OS version)
  • App performance and interaction with ads
  • Approximate location (derived from IP)

This collection is done by Google, not by us. We do not directly collect or store this advertising data.

AdMob may show personalized or non-personalized ads based on your device settings.

You can learn how Google uses this data here:

  • https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

You can control ads:

  • On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • You can opt-out of personalized ads in your Google account: https://myadcenter.google.com

4. No Other Tracking

Apart from AdMob described above, SparkQuote does not use analytics, crash reporting, or other tracking SDKs. We do not collect your email, precise location, contacts, or phone number.

5. Children's Privacy

SparkQuote is intended for users 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since ads are served by Google, we rely on Google's child-directed ad settings. If you are under 13, please use the app with parental guidance.

6. Data Control & Deletion

You can delete your name and all locally stored data at any time by clearing app data or uninstalling the app. Uninstalling permanently removes all local data from your device. To reset advertising data, use your device's ad settings.

7. Third-Party Services

The only third-party service used is:

  • Google AdMob (Ads)

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when we add new features or ad partners. We will update the "Last updated" date.
